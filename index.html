Good to know

A few things
before you go.

Every trip is a little different. Here are answers to the questions that often come up while planning.

How do I pay?

We’ll agree on a payment method after accepting your request. Mobile money, bank transfer, or cash at pickup may be available; your confirmation will explain the agreed details.

Is fuel included?

The daily rate is for the car itself. Fuel is usually full-to-full unless we agree something else in your confirmation email.

Can I cancel?

Please tell us as soon as plans change. If we have already confirmed the car, we’ll explain any fee before it applies. Pending requests can be withdrawn by emailing us with your reference.

Where can I pick up the car?

Pick-up options include Kampala, Entebbe (including the airport), and Jinja. A different return location can be selected in the request form; any related charge appears in your estimate.

What documents do I need?

A valid driving licence and a passport or national ID. If you would like a driver, you mainly need to be reachable at pickup.

Can I hire a driver?

Driver availability and cost vary by vehicle. Choose the driver option in the request form to include its listed cost in your estimate.
